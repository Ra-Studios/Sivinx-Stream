Complete User Guide

Sivinx Stream was designed with one singular goal: to remove the friction between human thought and digital text. We believe you should not have to context-switch, open a new application, or fumble with complex microphone settings just to transcribe your voice. Here is a comprehensive guide on exactly how to use the software to its maximum potential.

1. Initial Setup & Installation

After downloading the `.exe` installer from our official releases page, simply run the setup wizard. Sivinx Stream installs natively on your Windows machine in seconds. Once installed, it will automatically launch and place a sleek, unobtrusive icon in your System Tray (the bottom right corner of your Windows taskbar, near the clock).

2. The Settings Dashboard

You can right-click the Sivinx Stream icon in your system tray at any time to open the Settings dashboard. This is where you configure the brain of the application. We offer three distinct engines depending on your needs:

  • Deepgram Nova-2 (Cloud): The absolute bleeding edge of speed. Recommended for users with an active internet connection who want near-instantaneous transcription with exceptional punctuation and formatting.
  • Google Gemini (Cloud): The ultimate model for contextual understanding. Gemini excels at transcribing complex, multi-lingual, or highly technical jargon.
  • Whisper.cpp (Offline/Local): For absolute, uncompromised privacy. This model downloads directly to your hard drive and runs entirely on your local CPU/GPU. No audio data ever leaves your computer. Perfect for sensitive documents, medical records, or working on an airplane with no Wi-Fi.

3. The Push-to-Talk Philosophy

Unlike other transcription software that forces you to click a "Record" button and then click a "Stop" button, Sivinx Stream acts like a walkie-talkie. We use a global hotkey interceptor running at the operating system level.

  1. Click into ANY text field on your computer (a Microsoft Word document, a Discord chat, a web browser search bar, or an email draft).
  2. Press and hold down `CTRL + ALT` on your keyboard.
  3. While holding the keys, speak your thought naturally.
  4. Release the keys.

The moment you release the keys, Sivinx Stream instantly processes the audio buffer, queries the selected AI engine, and securely injects the perfectly punctuated text directly into wherever your typing cursor was located. It feels like magic.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need an internet connection to use Sivinx Stream? +
No. While the Deepgram and Gemini engines require an internet connection for cloud processing, Sivinx Stream comes with a fully functional Offline Mode powered by Whisper.cpp. You can transcribe infinitely with zero internet connection.
Does this work on Mac or Linux? +
Currently, Sivinx Stream is optimized specifically for Windows architectures (Windows 10 and 11) to ensure the deepest possible integration with the OS input layer. Cross-platform support is currently on our roadmap.
Is there a subscription fee? +
No. Sivinx Stream operates on a Bring-Your-Own-Key (BYOK) model for cloud services, and the offline local models are 100% free forever. We do not charge recurring subscriptions.
Can I change the CTRL + ALT hotkey? +
Yes, the hotkey is fully customizable within the Settings dashboard. You can bind it to any key combination that fits your personal workflow.

Privacy Policy

At RaStudios, privacy is not an afterthought; it is a fundamental architectural requirement. Because Sivinx Stream processes human voice recordings, we have structured the application to give you total control over your data.

  • Local Processing (Offline Mode): When using the offline Whisper models, your audio data is processed entirely within your computer's RAM. It is never saved to your hard drive, and it is never transmitted over the internet.
  • Cloud Processing: When utilizing Deepgram or Google Gemini, your audio buffer is securely transmitted to their respective API endpoints via encrypted TLS connections. We do not proxy, store, or log these requests on any RaStudios servers. The transaction is strictly between your machine and the AI provider.
  • Telemetry: Sivinx Stream does not contain tracking pixels, analytics SDKs, or background telemetry. We do not know when you use the app, how often you use it, or what you say.
